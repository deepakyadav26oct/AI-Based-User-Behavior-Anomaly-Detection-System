Theoretical & Architectural Analysis (Viva Defense):

Isolation Forest isolates outliers explicitly by taking advantage of two quantitative properties of anomalies: few in quantity and having attribute values very different from normal instances. In contrast, One-Class SVM attempts to construct a maximum-margin support vector boundary in Hilbert space, which suffers higher computational complexity on high-volume authentication streams and exhibits higher false-positive rates on mixed categorical telemetry.
